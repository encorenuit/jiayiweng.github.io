About Me
关于我

Always on Your Side: The Dual Pathways of Large Language Model Sycophancy in Shaping User Satisfaction
Jiayi Weng, et al.
Under review, Telematics and Informatics 在审，Telematics and Informatics CNAIS 2025 AOM 2026
Investigated how LLM sycophancy — the tendency of models to agree with users — shapes user satisfaction through dual pathways: perceived helpfulness and trust erosion. Used mixed-methods experiments with GPT-4 and Claude to reveal a non-linear relationship where moderate alignment increases satisfaction, but excessive sycophancy triggers distrust.
探究LLM谄媚行为——模型迎合用户倾向——如何通过「感知有用性」和「信任侵蚀」双重路径影响用户满意度。基于GPT-4和Claude开展混合方法实验，揭示了适度对齐提升满意度、过度谄媚反而引发不信任的非线性关系。
Tokenized Coordination for Data Supply Chain: A Blockchain-Based Mechanism Design with Agent-Based Simulation
Jiayi Weng, et al.
CSWIM 2026 (Accepted) CSWIM 2026（已录用） CSWIM 2026
Designed a tokenization mechanism for decentralized data supply chains, where data contributors are incentivized via smart contracts. Validated the mechanism through large-scale agent-based simulation (ABM), demonstrating improved coordination efficiency and fairness compared to traditional data marketplaces.
为去中心化数据供应链设计了代币化激励机制，数据贡献者通过智能合约获得激励。通过大规模多智能体仿真（ABM）验证了该机制在协调效率和公平性上优于传统数据交易平台。
